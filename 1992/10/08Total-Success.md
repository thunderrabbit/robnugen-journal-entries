#### 10:19 PM, Thursday 8 October 1992

TOTAL SUCCESS!!!

Today, I went to the VAX early to work on stuff before class. Gurlene had signed in just before I did. So, I sat next to
her. Lane saw me come in and sent me a mail immediately. I looked around and saw her a few rows away. Dude. So, I wrote
back to her and invited her to come sit next to me.

After a while, she did, and when she came up, she saw Gurlene. "Oh, hi, Gurlene. How are you doing?" Then Lane
introduced Gurlene and I, but we told her we already knew each other. So, Lane and I are sitting and talking about her
mountaineering class and life in general. Then, she went back to her terminal.

Gurlene turned around to face me with the biggest smile on her face, and she kind of grabbed my arm. "That's her," I
whisper to her. Gurlene said, "Do you know how long I've known her? Since high school." Dude. So, I wrote to Gurlene a
message like, "Okay, since you know her, does she have a boyfriend? Does she think I'm cute?"

As I was finishing the letter, Lane walked up again and saw her name on the mail so she thought it was to her. "Let me
read it." I told her it wasn't to her. Oh, what the heck. So I let her read it. Lane was all smiley, and after she read
it, we both totally didn't know what to say, and she pointed out, "Kind of awkward situation, eh?"

"Yep."

"Okay, when can I see you again?" I asked.

So, we set up a meeting at 8 pm. As she was leaving, I asked her, "Will you get mail before I see you again?"

"Yes."

In Bowling, I bowled 170. Excellent. My average is 107. Dude, and then check this out. I had taken my shoes back to the
counter and I was totally barefoot. I was about to put Ol' Blue away when I decided to do one last bowl: a slow motion
ball. I was standing on the tile floor entirely off the approach to lane 6. I set Ol' Blue on the approach just in
front of me and gave him a push. It was incredible. I was yelling, "Go, Blue, go, Blue, go!" Ol' Blue got a strike. I
couldn't believe it. That was the perfect slow motion ball. He was rolling about one meter per second. Amazing.
