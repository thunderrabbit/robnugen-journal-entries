Last night featured an interesting trigger for me after Jess and I were sitting in yab-yum.  After sitting for a bit, I let her lie back and was tracing fingertips on her body.  At first, she said she enjoyed it, but then soon said she was tired and wanted to sleep.

*ffs* I started thinking *it's only been a few minutes,* and I had been careful to only touch in safe zones.

I pouted and turned away from her; then felt angry that she was willing to stay awake to spend time talking about the situation instead of staying awake to enjoy sensations.
