After drafting an email in May 2025, I realized I can now run the self-sabotage coaching skill on it. What came up:

"I just want to be a magical happy boy doing whatever I want forever."

I traced the fear all the way back to childhood.
There's a sense of Mommy going away,
me being alone.
I project "Mommy" onto both Jess and Ms T.

For example, when Jess is happily talking:
she's good, so we're good, so I'm good.

Ms T not replying to emails make my brain think she's upset so I'm in trouble so I'm bad.  It feels devastating.

Cried. A lot. Good tears.

## The Email

After 10 months of sitting on it,
I finally wrote and rewrote a retainer proposal email.
¥220,000/month flat, covering the current rate
plus a share of the AI tooling cost I've been paying out of pocket.

I included a paragraph about the possibility of creating a
new revenue stream for AB using their existing data.

Also with AI support, I am able to do more work per hour than
I would be able to otherwise.
The retainer proposal solves that:
I won't need to wonder how many hours to charge for what.

I posted it to MKP Slack for feedback.
I haven't sent to Ms T yet.
