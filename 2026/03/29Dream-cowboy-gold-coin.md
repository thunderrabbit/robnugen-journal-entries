#### 06:17

I was caught up in some kind of cowboy movie, but an international conspiracy. I was in Mexico, witnessing murders happening — but only hearing about them, or hearing the actual gunshots happen. There was a large structure, like a mountain or tower on the right, and then down in the valley area there was a town where innocent people were killed. I basically ran away to stay alive. The killers had apparently killed the women instead of the men, and the men were going to kill them back.

Before all of that, there were robots that had a secret gold coin inside one of them. Upon the death of the owner of the gold coin, there was a puzzle for how to unlock the robot and find the gold coin hidden within it. That had been set up in a previous chapter — maybe a previous dream, because it seems like it was a continuation from a previous dream that I don't recall offhand. But I had the memory from a previous situation that these robots — maybe there was one robot that had a gold coin in it.

In today's dream, I was petting a cat and realizing that I could set up an even better way to hide the coin. There were two robotic cats and I wanted to set them up to reveal the gold coin upon the death of their owner. There were unsafe people beside me at the table, but then my dad was sitting across the table from me, and my stepdad — a man who I don't recognize at all, but he was a Japanese man sitting to my right.

Everybody was trying to find the golden coin ultimately, and I said, "Hey Dad, I have an idea." I was thinking about how to hide the gold coin, but instead of saying that, I said, "Are you hungry? Let's go get some food. I have an idea — let's go eat food," kind of thing. But it's like I almost revealed the idea of storing the gold coin inside a robotic cat — all this circuitous way to protect it.

When that scene ended, I was leaving there and got to — I don't remember how the transition happened, but I was then in the Mexican old west. There were people raiding a town and I was like, "Nope," and ran away.

I went toward the border. I saw some innocent-looking students who I thought I could trust, and I said, "Hey, I need to go." I was pointing out the window. "What happens if I go that way about two hours?" And they said, "You get to the city." Then I asked, "So that's the United States, right? That's America, right?" And they said yeah, and I said, "Cool, all right, see you guys." And they were like, "Oh, whatever."

So I went out and had to climb up — there was a barbed wire fence. I climbed over the fence at the same time that there was a kid running away from me and hiding in a small cupboard with a goat or something. I had no interest in even talking to her whatsoever, but she was afraid of me. When I kept going toward her, she hid, and I walked past her.

Then on the right, I could see some kind of scene happening with people trying to break through a border. There were horses fighting at the border. Then in some kind of almost comedy routine — because there was music and sound effects — they loaded up a human cannon. A woman with a hoop skirt was sitting on a very large spoon, and she was to be launched over. The launch was presented as if she's being flung across the border, but then there was a mistake and she only got launched on top of the person who was meant to flip the switch. She landed on top of him in this sexual tension situation that was supposed to be comedic.

I was like, "Okay, that's a lot of activity, and I'm not going to get mixed up into that." But I looked over on the left-hand side and I could see there was an open gate. I walked through it and ended up walking around the territory that was trying to be broken into by this comedic trebuchet-kind-of-spoon-launch situation.

I walked around that and immediately got to some railroad tracks with a train going to the left and another train going toward the right. These were freight trains. The second freight train was too short — just three cars long — for me to catch up to. So I was walking along the tracks waiting for the next train to come by.

Then I ended up getting onto a bus that had some participants in an international campaign — this was now modern day — to catch the bad guys. I said, "Hey, what if I told you I knew where the gold coin was?" And they said, "Oh really, let's do an interview right now." So they were beginning to style up the hair of one guy so he could interview me on TV. And as we began to prepare for the interview is when I woke up.
