#### 23:45

Yayyyy I'm so happy to show Marble Track 3 to Paul and Jo!  Thanks to Frase for mentioning it to our group at dinner.

I think Jo grokked the construction of the track most completely of anyone who I've introduced it to.  He noticed several different ways of looking at it, including:

* It's a pure endeavor in itself, utterly devoid of meaning.
* Time doesn't exist: the time spent for me to make stuff doesn't exist when people watch the video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KjsYc4Mb5g&list=PL0osPGt21FB48UwuhtCUoi9JndC9YDHAZ
* It shows a microcosm of life, with me as the higher power guiding the characters

We laughed and laughed at the insanity of it all. https://db.marbletrack3.com/ has lots of details, though is still nowhere near complete, having only 252 moments so far.
