#### 14:00

Today I visited the Telstra shop on Rundle Mall to get an OZ SIM card.
The staff member who helped me was very proficient and skilled.
She got the SIM sorted quickly and answered my questions,
including why I don't need to turn off the phone to change the SIM.

My understanding: think of it like a USB stick.

She's currently studying ICT (Information and Communication Technology)
and enjoys working at Telstra because it's in a similar industry.

When she had me test my phone,
I loaded my book page and showed her my book.
She seemed interested and might buy a copy.
I gave her my phone number with a true comment,
"you're the first person who knows this is my new number.."
and said if she buys a copy of my book I'll sign it for her.

I also accidentally gave her 56 JPY when
I was showing her Japanese coins and she thought they were a gift,
so they became a gift.
