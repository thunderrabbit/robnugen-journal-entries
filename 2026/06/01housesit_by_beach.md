<p>It's 4:30 a.m. on June 1st, 2026. Yesterday, Jess and I moved to our new housesit. This will be the last one that I'm in before I go back to Tokyo in the middle of this month. This is the one with an elevator that has no inner door.</p>

<p>We enjoyed walking along the beach and came across a very large pile of sand after walking for about 20 minutes at least. The sand is beautifully soft and fun to climb. But we didn't stay very long because we had to go back. I'll probably go back later today. After I do my morning meditation and stuff, maybe I can walk the dog there?</p>

<p>This beach has really soft sand and reminds me of Bondi Beach except there's seaweed on this beach because they don't have a beach cleaning truck that runs every night.</p>
