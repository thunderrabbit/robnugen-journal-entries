<p class="dream">7 A.M. June 1st.</p>

<p class="dream">I just had a dream that ended with holding space
for a woman who was organizing an event that I was attending.
We had started the first part of the workshop, or taking a
break of some sort, and I had written on the whiteboard that I
was going to start a podcast or to look for some free recording
software that was named Bobo, that had been introduced to me on
some video or something.</p>

<p class="dream">Was sitting on a? And someone sat down behind
me, leaning their back on my back for support. This person —
not fell down, but she lay down on her back and was saying, oh,
my support is gone. When I looked, it was my friend Mamiko, who
had been working on her project as well. And she was asking me
about something.</p>

<p class="dream">I couldn't understand her, so she got out some
kind of an old 1980s cassette tape player and asked me to record
into it. And I wasn't sure what she wanted me to record, but
then the organizer of the workshop came over to us and said,
like, Mary has left the workshop, where Mary is leaving the
workshop. And I told Mamiko in Japanese, ちょっと待て.</p>

<p class="dream">And then I asked the organizer if she needed
support. And she said yes, so we went into a nearby room that
had large glass doors. And I started listening to what she was
saying. Mary had left, and she was leaving because she hadn't
known that there was an assigned reading to be done before the
workshop.</p>

<p class="dream">Then I was starting to wake up as I realized
the next question that I might ask, if she thought that was the
actual reason that Mary had left, or if there was something
else, but I was waking up at that time.</p>
