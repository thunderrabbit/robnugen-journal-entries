I like having the timezone appended to my journal entries; it gives a little hint as to my location in the world while writing the entry.  I was in Adelaide since about September 8th to a couple days ago when Jess and I came to Sydney for 3 weeks, so any entries between 8th September and about 10 minutes ago that say "JST" should probably be updated.
