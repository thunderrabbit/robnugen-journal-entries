#### 05:00

We all have different types of intelligence. My aunt Madeline would say,
instead of asking, "how smart are you?" She should begin asking, "how are you
smart?" I would say, "what are the ways that you have been gifted?" or
"What are your superpowers?"

A fish doesn't know that they have a
superpower of breathing underwater. Most fish probably don't even know
that they live underwater from our perspective. Given how deep the ocean
is, most fish probably don't even know that they live underwater in the way
that we think about it. If they could come up to the surface, they might
be amazed that we can breathe in.

I think about what do people come to me for? What do people
ask me? These questions lean toward, point me toward my superpowers. In
my case, I often thought one of my superpowers is listening.

Not only do I tend to not speak up, but I enjoy listening. For example,
I love listening to Jess and holding space for her. She loves
verbalizing because she is what they call an external thinker.
