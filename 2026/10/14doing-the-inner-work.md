#### 01:14

    Doing the inner work is like helping a friend out of a jam. In
    this case, the friend is your younger self who began this life and
    was inadvertently jammed up with all types of beliefs and culture
    ... perhaps events that were challenging and never had a chance to
    be fully processed or even acknowledged. Acknowledging these and
    having some empathy for these events helps your inner friend, your
    younger self, out of the backlog of all the stuff that he or she
    has experienced in life, allowing you to remember more fully who
    you are.
