The short version:

> I once recovered a past life where I had committed ritual suicide
> because I was shamed and I lived as a sad ashamed ghost for
> uncountable years until in this lifetime, I re-encountered that
> aspect of my soul and my human guide said "look up."  I looked up
> and all my angels were waving and trying to get my attention and I
> just burst into tears because I had put myself into hell of
> isolation unnecessarily.

From [Life in a Post as of July 2019](/journal/2019/07/22/state-of-my-life-address/),
written to Dean Ramsden:

> I would be curious to do so as I remember one of my experiences as a
> ghost believing I did not deserve to go through the tunnel.

and later in the same post:

> In a different past life (which I mentioned above), I committed ritual
> suicide and remained on earth believing my transgression had been too
> great to let me go to 'heaven'.  The facilitator (Kevin Turner, who
> you may well know) said "look up" and my dead-self looked up and was
> shocked to see everyone waiting patiently for me.  My physical self
> started bawling and crying like oh my god finally, etc etc.

From [AM psychopomp](/journal/2010/03/28/AM_psychopomp/), 28 March 2010:

> My understanding of how it works: a human dies, but doesn't know
> they're dead (or doesn't know how (or that they deserve) to cross over
> for shower and debriefing).  Their guardian angels are there to help
> them cross over, but, being at such a higher level of resonance, they
> are invisible to the human soul that's focused on its own troubles.
> But the soul can see me because I'm still at crude human level of
> resonance.  So the simple task is to help the soul see his/her angels,
> simply by shifting their awareness just a bit.  Then they're like,
> "oh!" and they handle everything from there.
