<p class="dream">There was an elevator in this building where my ex-wife's family lived on about five or six different floors. And I was trying to figure out how to leave Japan and what to do with all my stuff and seeing what I could give them. So I was trying to go up and find her brother-in-law who goes to Taiwan and talk to him. And when I got to, on the way to his floor, the elevator was showing lots of numbers, including like prices on the screen instead of what floor it was going to go to. And when I finally got to his floor, he welcomed me in and we were going to talk about something when I woke up.</p>
