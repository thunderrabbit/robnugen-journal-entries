#### 11:16

Yesterday, I had a good time at the Reiki level one training. There
were five attendees, some of whom will go to the Reiki level 2 that
happens 3 weeks from now! I knew both of the trainers, Jody and
Kalisha, and they did a good job of facilitating the training and
giving us some practical, literal hands-on time to practice offering
Reiki.

This is my second time to be Reiki trained. The first time was in
Japan, something like 20 years ago, in Japanese language. So this
time it's interesting to basically understand everything that's being
said, although there's still some Japanese words, and this time I'm
able to fix the pronunciation of the words that had gotten
transliterated into English, and hopefully that was helpful.

But the other training was great, and I came home and shared with
Jess my experience, and we compared it to her experience attending
the same training with him a few, maybe a month ago. And Jess and I
are both looking forward to attending level on June 14th, the last
day of my travel here in Adelaide.
