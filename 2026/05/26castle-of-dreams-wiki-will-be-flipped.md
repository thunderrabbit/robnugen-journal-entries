Since no later than 2009, I have been bugged by the Castle of Dreams entrance facing the North.  My sense has always been that the entrance should face South. Today AI determined that the room names in the castle were generally consistent with a Castle in England, even going so far as to say it's likely in Wales.

Therefore, unless there's a mountain protecting it from cold northern winds, its entrance should definitely face South.

Therefore x 2, Imma gonna flip the Castle so we arrive from the South and enter while traveling North.

This will require adjusting three sets of things:

1. Navigation instructions attached to each room with my plugin
2. Wikitext description of each room
3. Names of (some) rooms that include cardinal directions

Regarding item う, I don't yet know if I'll keep East and West rooms the same during The Great Flippening, or if I'll also swap East and West in The Great Rotate.

PS: as of last night, the Castle of Dreams wiki is at the latest LTS version of Mediawiki, 1.43.
