<p class="dream">I was with people from Ultimate Frisbee Community,
including Mike and maybe some others, and we had this idea of making
some kind of a time capsule sort of as a joke for someone's birthday
as well. At one point I dug a very small hole and buried some keys
inside a rock, and buried it into the ground. Then later I came back
to the same spot and dug a deeper hole in a square shape that was
about half an arm's length deep.</p>

<p class="dream">I had put the dirt on the side there. Interestingly,
there was actually a brick framing of the hole — somehow I had dug
perfectly inside this brick framing, although I didn't know it was
there at the time that I started taking. I had used a sharp thin rock
to dig out the square outline, and it was exactly within this brick
framing, which was interesting. I left the hole there and went — no,
I don't know, to do something.</p>

<p class="dream">I was in a different segment of dream, riding my
bicycle. In a different segment of my dream I was on a late night
train — oh man, there's so much. I had gone so far out that I wasn't
going to be able to get back home that night, so I would have to stay
overnight at this location. When I got off the train, I realized I
had somehow left my phone on the train.</p>

<p class="dream">My friend Jacob Anderson was on the train. He was
there with his girlfriend, and I got off and — oh no, I left my
phone, but it was too late to get it. And I wondered if he would find
it, if he would, and if he would reply to me via email or something
because he couldn't text me because I didn't have my phone.</p>

<p class="dream">I arrived at this large mansion-type place that had
some people preparing for some kind of a concert. It was in Japan,
and I thought, oh gosh, it actually looks kind of fun and I should
make the best of it while I'm here. I did have my wallet, but I
didn't have my phone. So I thought, okay, so I went to what looked
like the front desk come to place and was like, hey, you know what,
what's going on here? And they confirmed, it's like, it's for a
concert. And I asked who would be performing to see if it's anyone
that I knew of.</p>

<p class="dream">They said a couple of names which seem like
nicknames. One was like Ministry, but it wasn't just the word
Ministry — it was something ministered like Stone Ministry or
something. And I was like, thinking it sounds tantalizingly close to
a band that I had heard of, but they didn't list any of my favorite
artists. They did not list any of my favorite artists, so I asked
how much the tickets were, and I said it in dollars — you know, are
they $80 or how much are they? And they looked at me like, asking
what am I talking about. Because it would be priced in Yen. But then
they didn't give me a clear answer on the price. They kind of said,
you know, of course it's not eighty dollars. I said, oh, what about
8,000 Yen? Of course it's not 8,000, and what are you talking about
— but they didn't tell me what the actual number is, so that was
funny.</p>

<p class="dream">Then I was riding my bicycle away from the area. I
didn't end up going to the concert. Oh, it was dark — I remember it
was dark, and I could barely see where I was going, so I turned on
the light of my bicycle. I crossed the street and got to a
construction area where there was a giant gaping hole in the road,
which was a meter by a meter square. I stopped and looked down in
the hole — it was an entire room, with chairs stacked inside.</p>

<p class="dream">It was still part of this birthday joke time capsule
thing. They had trying to figure out what else to put down there.
It was large enough that we could practically have a party down in
that room — definitely could do something like you play duck duck
goose on all the chairs and stuff. I was there with Maruan, my
friend from traveling days back in Japan. And I said it reminds me
of the time that we took a picture somewhere, and I said, oh, no,
that was with Matt. Yeah, it reminds me of the time you and I were
splashing in the hot tub when they were draining the water out of
the hot tub. Yeah, I think I woke up soon thereafter.</p>
