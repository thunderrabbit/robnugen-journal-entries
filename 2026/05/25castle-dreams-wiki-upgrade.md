#### 16:56

AI and I are in the middle of upgrading my domain, Thunderrabbit.com, where I used MediaWiki to recreate the old BBS Castle of Dreams as best I could. The Castle of Dreams was in existence before the World Wide Web as we know it, and was basically in pure text-based system with no images to speak of, so I think it's a perfect fit to recreate on a Wiki.

The sysop of Castle of Dreams said that they basically lost all of the data from those days — basically the database. Somehow the old copies were rolled off the end of the thing; once something happened and it started to write empty copies to the DB or something. But I somewhere was able to find some of the old text and I have used it, for example, on what I called the Dragon Runway and in the main hall entrance of the castle, so a few bits are still kept from those pre-internet days. That would be like 1995, 1994, 96 — no, even earlier, 93, 1992. Gosh, so it's been a minute. But anyway, I'm about to get this thing upgraded to version 1.43 right now.

AI is upgrading to the more modern version of MediaWiki, my plugin, which creates links out of a tag called navigation, and the links come from a kind of a database — although it's just a data structure built into the plugin. It needs eventually to be its own actual database. For now, it'll be good just to have AI support to get the plugin modernized so it will work with the current version of MediaWiki 1.39.3, and soon will be 1.39.9, and then to version 1.43 point whatever it is now.

It's 4:56 p.m. as I record this and I'm going to have a meeting with Yuki now for about an hour as we work on chapter 10 of the book, Build Yourself up Without Limits. Hopefully we can make a workshop series out of the content in the book.
