#### 2 am

<p class="dream">I was facing someone. Maybe it's like a spiritual someone,
or someone who was aware that I was dreaming. Was inviting me to
wake up within the dream. I could. I knew I was dreaming, and I
could see him clearly, even though I was dreaming. And there was
something like a fear that I would lose my consciousness, and he
asked, what? What happened? Why can't you stay? Not in a pleading
way, but in a coaching way. He was asking me what's taking you away,
trying to encourage me to be able to stay focused.</p>

<p class="dream">And I was able to see him clearly. I realized I was
dreaming and not quite wake up. I was saying something like, when I
focus too much, I end up back in my physical body and lose track of
the dream. I end up back in my physical body and lose track of the
dream. But I was able to stay with him for a little bit while I was
aware of dreaming.</p>

<p class="dream">Then I looked at a tree and tried to focus on different
things while I was dreaming, not just his face, but looking at a tree
or different landscapes that I was seeing.</p>
