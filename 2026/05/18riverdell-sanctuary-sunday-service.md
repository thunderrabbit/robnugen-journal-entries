#### 13:00

After Jess's workshop, we went to the sanctuary for the
Sunday service. And then went to the morning tea on Sunday.
After the service, the emissaries who have been running
Riverdell for basically decades. (I think the Emissaries
started in 1945 in United States, and Riverdell was started
in 1974 something here in Adelaide.)

Yesterday after the Sunday service they offered a thing
called Attunement, which is something like Reiki healing
energy focused on the different glands in the body. That was
new for me and Jess, so that was nice. And Kathryn and Ruth
each bought a copy of my book. They had expressed interest
in buying a copy before. Raymond was telling us about the
history of Riverdell from his understanding and his
perspective. So that was very interesting to hear some of
the backstory of how Riverdell has come to be formed.

After that, Jess and I had lunch here in the Loft apartment.
Which basically overlooks the Dell. Beautiful ❤️
