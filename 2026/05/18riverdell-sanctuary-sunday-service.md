#### 13:00

After Jess's Workshop, we went to the Sanctuary for
the Sunday service, and then went to the morning tea
on Sunday after the service. The emissaries have been
running Riverdell for basically decades.

(Actually, I think the Emissaries started in 1945 in
United States, and Riverdell was started in 1974
something here in Adelaide.)

Yesterday after the Sunday service they offered a
thing called Attunement, which is something like Reiki
healing energy focused on the different glands in the
body. That was new for me and Jess. So that was nice,
and we then went to the morning tea.

Kathryn and Ruth each bought a copy of my book. They
had expressed interest in buying a copy before Raymond
was telling us about the history of Riverdell from his
understanding and his perspective.

So that was very interesting to hear some of the
backstory of how Riverdell has come to be formed.

After that, Jess and I had lunch here in the loft
apartment which basically overlooks the dell. Beautiful ❤️
