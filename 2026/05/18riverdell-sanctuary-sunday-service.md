#### 13:00

After Jess's Workshop, we went to the Sanctuary for the Sunday
service, then to the morning tea after the service. The
Emissaries who have been running Riverdell for basically decades
(they started in 1945 in the United States; Riverdell was started
in 1974 in Adelaide).

After the Sunday service they offered a thing called Attunement,
which is like Reiki healing energy focused on the different glands
in the body. That was new for me and Jess, so that was nice.

At the morning tea, Kathryn and Ruth each bought a copy of my
book. They had expressed interest before. Raymond was telling us
about the history of Riverdell from his understanding and
perspective, which was very interesting to hear.

After that, Jess and I had lunch here in the Loft apartment, which
basically overlooks the Dell. Beautiful ❤️
