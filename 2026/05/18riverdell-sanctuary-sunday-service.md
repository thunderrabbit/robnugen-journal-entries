#### 13:00

1pm Sunday, May 17, 2026.

After Jess's Workshop, we went to the Sanctuary for the Sunday service, and then went to the morning tea on Sunday after the service. The emissaries who have been running Riverdell for basically decades.

(I think the Emissaries started in 1945 in United States, and Riverdell was started in 1974 something here in Adelaide.)

Yesterday after the Sunday service they offered a thing called Attunement, which is something like Reiki healing energy focused on the different glands in the body. That was new for me and Jess, so that was nice. And we then went to the morning tea.

And Kathryn and Ruth each bought a copy of my book. They had expressed interest in buying a copy before. Raymond was telling us about the history of Riverdell from his understanding and his perspective, so that was very interesting to hear some of the backstory of how Riverdell has come to be formed.

After that, Jess and I had lunch here in the Loft apartment, which basically overlooks the Dell. Beautiful ❤️
