<p class="dream">6:00 a.m. Dream.</p>

<p class="dream">I invited Jess to join me for a weekly meeting of
connection and check-in and was having trouble figuring out how to
create a new account for her and system, partially because I couldn't
see the screen properly. And I had accidentally mistyped the name Anna
with two A's and one N, so this search wasn't working properly and I
was confused about what was happening on the laptop.</p>

<p class="dream">The meeting was ongoing and I realized that I should
have done this prep work earlier if I really wanted it to go smoothly,
but here we were in real time having a fight about her feeling not
connected while I was in the middle of the meeting, specifically
trying to help her feel connected.</p>

<p class="dream">The meeting ended at 10 A.M. and because of the
kerfuffle I had not finished packing my stuff by 10:00 a.m. and was
trying to print a few things, so I went to the regular print station.
Japanese security guard pointed and said over, who was pointing to
what I knew to be the wrong location for printers. I felt confused and
frustrated, and then I realized he was meaning well because he knew
that I would know how to use the printers, and these two wrist
printers were being closed.</p>

<p class="dream">I understood his meaning and then went back to collect
my things, which were seeming to expand in number and too much room to
carry all at one time. Some friends of mine went by — they were moving
a couch with one person on the couch who offered to put my stuff on
the couch so it can be carried as well, and I basically said yes, that
would be great, I'm going up to level two over there. And they were,
like, oh well, we're going up to level nine. So the implication was
that they could not help, which was confusing for me, but just
accepted and said, no problem.</p>

<p class="dream">Then one of my friends who was about to go on a voyage
around the world was there, and I gave her a hug. Kind of cry and
whispered, I've had such a bad morning. She made some empathetic
noises. And I said, but I'll be okay and I have the resources I need.
I said, I hope you have a beautiful trip, and I look forward to seeing
you when you get back.</p>
