<p class="dream">I had basically been caught by some goons. The
shadowy back office of some large corporation. They had detected
my heartbeat beating quickly through a door, and as I was
realizing that they could probably detect me and trying to leave
the building where I had just gone to seek shelter briefly.
Opened the door as I was trying to leave and were asking me
questions about what I was doing there. Tried to intimidate me
by saying they wanted to do a cavity check. I was completely
nonchalant, somehow knowing that they couldn't hurt me. When I
realized it was a dream, I just slowly faded away from their
hold and had the last laugh.</p>

<p class="dream">Later, Jess and I were at a nice hotel as part of
a retreat. When one of the attendees had a question or issue with
her room, the hotel staff woman who came was the same one who had
talked to Jess and I, maybe a few days ago, about the fragrances
of animals in the beach or the fragrance of very bro culture of
masculine beach boy type guys on the beach? And I was surprised
that Jess recognized the person out of context, but it indeed was
her. We had a little chat and she sorted out the person who had
the question.</p>
