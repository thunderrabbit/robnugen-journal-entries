<p class="dream">6 a.m.</p>

<p class="dream">May 21st. In my dream, I was in a classroom
with something like multi-level marketing. Back when I was
in Amway back in my university days, there was one particular
speaker who I really loved. When he came out onto stage, he
had been hyped up so much that I actually remember crying when
he came out. It was like, wow.</p>

<p class="dream">In this dream he came out, but this time
into a classroom, not like a whole stadium full of people.
He came into his classroom and there were about 20 people
behind different desks. I commented to someone who was
standing in the back of the room that when this guy came
out, when I first saw this guy, I cried because I was so
impressed. The man commented back something like he could
imagine me having a downline as large as Adelaide or
something. And I'm just like, hmm.</p>

<p class="dream">In this reality, I'm definitely not in any
multi-level marketing. Although it would be nice to, for
example, sell as many books as people in Adelaide. In the
dream I went to the front of the room to sit near the front
table. There were a few people crowding around the front
table, all women by the way.</p>

<p class="dream">I was sitting up there looking for paper in
my notebook and didn't have any, but I did have some paper
related to maybe some previous inspirational notes I had
written, and some types of little slips of paper, something
like handmade coupons or something. I woke up soon after
that.</p>
