<p class="dream">My dream was related to a group of people who liked playing video games.
And there was a game which was very much like a sandbox game using SVG type graphics that
were clear, infinitely expandable.</p>

<p class="dream">I wasn't really used to the controls of the game, so didn't know how to
play very well, but there were some worlds which I had played, taking books out of a giant
library of bookshelves in order to choose which level we would play, and then we would maybe
dive into the book or something.</p>

<p class="dream">I had this idea I wanted to play with the pun, Lichen subscribe. And I had
this idea of having that pun be encoded into the game somehow.</p>

<p class="dream">There was also something about driving in my dream, but I can't quite
capture more than the idea of cars off in the distance near a parking lot.</p>
