#### 06:15

Yesterday we walked to Kris and Mike's house for yummy lunch
that Kris made and had a nice long chat with them over lunch.
Talking about travel stories, especially when Kris and I were on
P 49 and talking about all of our friends and where they have gone
since then. She seems to be in touch with several people, but
realistically I'm only in touch with her out of the crowd.

Afterward we played Uno with their son—five of us played a quick game.
And then Mike drove us to our next meet and greet that we were
going to do in Norwood which is about an 11-minute drive away.

We met Elizabeth and her husband and their dog, Charlie, and got
to go through the details of the house and then we walked to the
bus stop and with perfect timing arrived there just as the bus was
arriving. So I was very grateful for the nice synchronicity,
on time, divine timing. We took the bus back to where we're
staying with Suzi, the standard poodle.

When we got back, she had eaten her food.
