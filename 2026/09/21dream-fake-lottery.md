<p class="dream">2.30 a.m. dream. There was a man who was trying to sell some fake lottery tickets by claiming that we could win lots of money. He had somebody call his office while we were there. He picked up the phone and said, oh wow, the two million dollar home was sold or something, was given away. That's amazing. Then he hung up immediately. And I realized that if it were a real call, he wouldn't have hung up immediately after that. So I knew that it was a sham. And I recommended that we give up on that.</p>
