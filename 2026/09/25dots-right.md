#### 25 September

I vibe-coded a version of Dots based on my old
Pascal version, and it's now on my website at https://www.robnugen.com/dots/.
And we even vibe-coded it so that people could play a game
over the internet. It uses Perl to receive the messages and keeps
track of the state of the game through JSON files in a directory
that's not accessible via the web.

I'm happy it worked well enough that I
was able to send a link to Maiku who happened to be awake and we
played a quick game. I used a trick to beat him quite handily at
the game, but I did leave him a few squares that he could capture
at the end. We were texting online at the time. He said he has no
idea how he didn't beat me. And I said, I use an old Chinese
secret. And he was like, whoa, I didn't know you were Chinese,
bro. It's very funny. We're thinking about creating a series of
little mini games on a website somewhere.
