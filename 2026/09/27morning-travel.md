#### 05:42

I woke up around 5:30 AM and just spoke my two dreams into the
phone and my computer is already packed and ready to go. I'm about
to take a shower and then do meditation and be plenty of time
before I need to go to the next house. I need to leave at 8:30, so I
still have nearly three hours until then. There's a small chance
that I'll just pack all my stuff and take another nap,
but I'm going to make sure I'm totally packed first. That means just
packing the final bits of clothing that were wet and hanging last
night. I checked and see my jeans are already dry and it was just
the red flannel shirt that was not yet dry. Right now it's shower
time.

I've already hidden a snack for Jess inside the kitchen cabinet
with a vertical sliding drawer door, and I hid a cookie from Bio c'
Bon in there with a little note underneath it expressing my love for
her. So she'll get that when she feeds the dog today. After coming
out and finding no cookie on the counter, which is where I had been
leaving them at this house and the previous house, one per day. I
think I still have seven little snacks remaining for her, so they
will not last the whole trip, but I've been enjoying pretending to
not know where they came from each time she finds them.
