#### 16:47

Yesterday, I went to Mustard Seed Church for a
conversation event titled Why Are Humans Attracted
to Nature?, which to me seems like a tautology of
sorts because humans are part of nature and are
attracted to things that are natural so I was like,
wait, that, what, however it was interesting to talk
to a couple of people who said that one guy said his
partner did not like anything related to nature and
well might be able to enjoy witnessing a sunset but
only from inside a building or something. It was
interesting to talk about how I have come to believe
in the spiritual world around us by starting with
gratitude and finding that my life has become better
by expressing gratitude and having more things
arrive in my life that I have to be grateful for. So
it's been a process but that conversation was
something like a synchronicity or a lot like a
synchronicity.

Because just yesterday Yuki and I had our weekly
call and I was sharing with her about something I
don't remember but she was taking notes and at the
end of what I said she's like, wow Rob, this is so
great you should record this and post it on
Instagram or something. And I was like, oh really,
oh my gosh, and intellectually I can see yes it's
valuable but in my feeling body, I'm like, oh, I'm
so scared of putting myself out there, so to speak.

So I'm like, okay, I need to, I need to do this
because I need to step through my fear and feel so
afraid of ridicule from my scientific beloved
scientific friends and family and my whole upbringing
I was like totally poo-pooing religion but now I'm
finding myself saying all the same stuff that I was
hearing as a kid that I need to have belief in God
and blah blah blah. But the difference from me, I
don't have to believe in a specific brand name of
anyone and I don't believe that there is any sin, we're
not going to go to hell if we get things wrong.
Although in a way we are already in hell here on
earth and anything that we do better is like getting
ourselves out of hell.

But I also talk about how we live in a physical
reality for a reason. So in the same way that I
believe emotions are important, I believe living in
physical reality is important because we're here by
definition and we're naturally drawn to physical
reality and so therefore we need to express ourselves
in physical reality.

So these are some of the things I'm going to start
exploring today. Yuki and I have a call at 4:30
today, I'll start recording some of this stuff and
yeah start putting myself out there. So yeah these
will be my first steps and first foray into putting
myself out there stepping through the fear and such.
Thank you.
