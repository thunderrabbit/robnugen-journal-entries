I found a little blue shoe sitting all alone. About 100 metres later, I found its long-lost partner. I reunited the two, and now they're sole mates again.

[![2026 sep 20 blue sole mates 1000](//b.robnugen.com/journal/2026/thumbs/2026_sep_20_blue_sole_mates.jpeg)](//b.robnugen.com/journal/2026/2026_sep_20_blue_sole_mates_1000.jpeg)

After walking past the first shoe, I saw a Foodland tarp that had about 12 boxes of bananas waiting for someone to take them away.  I asked one of the guys sitting there if he needed help getting rid of the bananas.  "Yep. You can take as many as you want."
