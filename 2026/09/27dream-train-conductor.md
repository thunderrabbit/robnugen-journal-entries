navigation when to change trains or when to get off. I was talking to one
of the train conductors who was pouring coal with a small shovel into a
chute. But this was on the side of the train, nothing near the front. It
was just a random train car I was on. And she was on the side of the
train pouring like coal crumbs, they were very small crumbs, like with a
scoop, maybe like granola sized crumbs of coal into a chute on the side
of train and she was telling me something but I don't remember</p>
