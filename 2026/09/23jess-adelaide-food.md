#### 16:00

Jess and I went to get some food at
Zambrero's. It was a little bit spicier than I would like, but
it was okay. I have this kind of emotional connection to
Zambreros because we enjoyed it on one of my first trips into
Adelaide, but it hasn't been as good since then. We also went
and bought a card for Kris from p 49, which I filled out and included a
link to my days old counter. We also found a discount health
food store which had a very nice cashier. He was helpful and
told us that tomorrow meaning September 24th being the last
Thursday of the month they'll have a sale so we'll come back
Thursday to buy some stuff. We also stopped by a Chinese
grocer and Jess thinks that she'll make us Chinese food after
we finish the current food that we bought at Kohl's. Mostly we
bought salad type stuff and some oat milk.
