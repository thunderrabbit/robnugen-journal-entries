<p class="dream">I was working at a police station helping people pick up
their belongings when they got out of jail. There was a
table with guys behind it giving out little pieces of
slips of paper with short codes on them.</p>

<p class="dream">An item labeled CF was ready to be picked up. A woman came
from jail looking for a wallet, but the person was supposed
to be named Walter. She said her name was loose or Louise,
and the last name wasn't Walter. Even though it was almost
a match, it wasn't a match. I just remained silent and said
I had no idea.</p>

<p class="dream">I asked for Heather, who was my boss, kind of the
coordinator. Heather talked to her, but it wasn't my
person. It was supposed to be someone with last name Walter.</p>
