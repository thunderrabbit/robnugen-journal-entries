#### 14:09

I'm walking to the bus stop after eating at Cafe Fix with
Phoenix Ultimate peeps after playing Ultimate with the
guys here (in North Adelaide).

Jess and I have eaten at Fix before because we house sat in
the St. Peter's area. It was interesting to go somewhere
that I've already been before.

Phoenix Ultimate community is great. We had
easily 12 people at the cafe. We almost continuously had seven
on seven players with about four subs total, so 22 people
available to play, and had a great time.

I'm really happy to
find this group. The level of play is higher than the play in
Yoyogi Park for our pickup games but everyone is still friendly
and encouraging even if mistakes happen so I really enjoyed it.

I will very likely attend again on each Saturday
that I'm available in Adelaide.  Really happy and
looking forward to more!
