the guys in Harajuku. They were like, where's your shoes and stuff?

been barefoot for that long. And your feet are not tough enough to

with it. I was like okay but it also made me feel like I wish I
could prove it. I can prove that I was barefoot for so long but it
doesn't matter so yeah just interesting to have that reaction from
someone.
