<p class="dream">One of my friends was visiting his girlfriend in prison
and I needed to go in with a meal voucher for myself that I had to hide
inside my shoe. We were at a dinner with the several important people
including the former president. And I asked them why I had to sneak the
meal voucher in because they had found out later that I did it through my
shoe. They said, the other option, if I get the meal voucher the previous
day at this facility, the only other option is to stay inside and not go
outside.</p>

<p class="dream">Obama said that he would enjoy that option and just stay
up on the roof and play golf and watch the sunset. I said, it sounds like
a rough life, Mr. President, but no laughter and no reaction. What? We
went back down to the room where my friend was being kept on kind of
house arrest. and she wasn't in there when we knocked on the door. But
then her boyfriend arrived and she arrived and they were about to speak
when I woke up.</p>
