<p class="dream">
I was beginning to think about having the recording
function be built into the app and let it run all night
long so I could just speak and have the sound detection
and actually record the sound with a little bit of a
back buffer. I was thinking about Lek, the woman from
Elephant Sanctuary, recently having her videos on social
media showing how she's living her life with elephants.
I met her 15 years ago at Elephant Sanctuary, so she's
obviously dedicated her entire life since she was a
girl—she received an elephant as a kid and her life has
been toward saving elephants. She has a sanctuary in
Thailand. I was thinking about what I dedicate my life
to and if consciousness exploration could be it. There
was some kind of idea of a structure back to my
bicycle—a structure that could move up and down, maybe
something like getting into a physical capsule when
going to sleep.
</p>

<p class="dream">
I heard a sound come from my phone, which was a literal
physical sound. I'm going to switch my phone to airplane
mode. To be clear, the audio happened because my phone
was unplugged since I've been doing these different
recordings. Now I'm going to plug in my phone and it
will switch to do-not-disturb mode.
</p>

<p class="dream">
I was again dreaming of recording a dream, which I had
been thinking about how Tom Bilyeu said that governments
tend to lose the things that they tax. When states are
taxing billionaires, they tend to lose billionaires. I
read some text and read it again. I just heard the
audio cue to read some text and read it again. I'm
looking at August 28th. I closed my eyes, it opened my
eyes, it still says August 28th. That counts as a
lucidity check. I was thinking about things that are
taxed tend to go away and could see a large structure,
like an edifice, maybe a government building trying to
do something but actually doing the exact opposite. I
considered recording that by just speaking without
having to turn to my left to click the record button.
Then I realized I was dreaming about recording.
</p>

<p class="dream">
I was standing at the edge of a road where there was a
wall between the sidewalk and the road and I needed to
step into the road. A Japanese person expressed in
Japanese incredulously: 入れるの (Can it be entered?).
I don't know how the transcription will take it, but it
means "Can it be entered?" with an incredulous marker.
He was surprised it was even possible to enter the road.
</p>

<p class="dream">
Before that I was wondering if I had recorded. The
architecture of the app would be optimal if it could
record with a buffer. I could connect to the same Google
Drive and save recordings, which could both be parsed
by HairBrush first, who cleans up the audio, and then
Carrie can put it into my journal. But then because
they are dreams, they could also have the timestamps
included, so I don't have to keep saying the timestamp
and I don't have to keep turning over to see the
timestamp or click. I was thinking about how this might
or might not work with me being in Australia with Jess
starting in about 12 days. I was thinking about my
financial situation and her plans for our first few
days when we get there. I was thinking about how
exploration of consciousness could help people who want
to be more aware of different states of reality and
wondering about ways of exploring reality that might be
useful for others. That part is my financial mind
thinking about things. My heart is just saying keep
exploring what I enjoy and keep praying for support
from guides and higher power.
</p>

<p class="dream">
I was at an outdoor picnic type thing while watching
some videos of me teaching and I was really unimpressed
with how I had been doing it at that time. The
recordings included me doing some voices, mumbling words
and not enunciating very well. It was ostensibly a
teaching session and the version of me in the video was
getting frustrated with people, including my friend
Will from men's group. I was listening to it in the
dream, shaking my head, thinking how bad it was and
looking forward to it being over. I was sitting in a
chair at the same place and I could see a porch where
two of my friends were standing—two men who I know from
Tokyo, but not from men's group. They were two black men
who I know, but I can't think if I know them in physical
reality or just from dream reality. They were friends of
mine and one was playing guitar, one's doing vocals.
They started to take photographs of me and I was
self-conscious because my erection was poking up out of
my shorts so it could be seen above my shorts. I did
some poses to cover it so it wouldn't be recorded on
film. One of them came up close to me and took a
portrait photo of me, then they took off my glasses and
asked me to do one more without my glasses, like the
Vikings in the summer perhaps. There was a crowd on
the outside of a stage and an MC talking to people.
There was a woman on the shoulders of a man, both
wearing summer time Viking costumes and speaking in a
language I didn't understand. At one point the man held
her bare breast affectionately and gropingly, although
it wasn't explicit—more like spring break party vibes.
Then I moved away from the scene and went back to a
building where I could see the scene from afar. It
became 6:00 p.m. during the party and I left. When I
looked up again, the party was entirely gone. Five
minutes later, everybody left the area and there was no
one left there. That was surprising enough that it
could have been a dream sign, but I didn't notice in
the dream. For the record I have only heard a couple of
the dream cues tonight. I might make the audio a little
bit louder. Let me try again tonight. I think I have
slept my seven and a half hours, but I will lay here a
little bit longer. I kind of remember there was a
previous long dream but I can't recall details at the
moment. There was some kind of intrigue or espionage or
something but I can't recall details.
</p>
