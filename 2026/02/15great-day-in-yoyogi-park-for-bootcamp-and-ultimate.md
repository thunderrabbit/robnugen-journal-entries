#### 22:32pm Sunday 15 February 2026

I'm super happy, super exhausted after a super fantastic great day today at yoyogi Park for Bootcamp and Ultimate 🥏.

There were only two regular leaders for Bootcamp so I led the third section. It was funny when Kyoko and a couple of others were smiling saying "oh, you're a secret leader!" Because I only lead the group if no one else is available. 

I don't know where the other leaders were 🤔 today's weather was fantastic so there must have been some other event going on.

For the first couple of hours playing ultimate, I had excellent (for me) running speed and was very high energy.  

I talked to the group who was standing on the sidewalk watching us for a while and invited them to play. A man named Mark from Gold Coast played with us for a few points. I felt happy seeing his joy and watching him catch a point and throw a point at various times. 

We also had a young boy named Antony join us. He was really fast and seemed like he had played before but his mom said he just naturally picked it up.

Today was Jack's last day with us. He's only played 2 weeks but I really took a liking to his friendly playing style. He said he tries to play the way he would want to have others play with him.

For example, when he was covering me and I was running full tilt to catch the disc in the end zone, instead of really challenging me for the disc, he just said "you're safe," to indicate that there was no one about to get accidentally clobbered by me.

I really appreciate his playing spirit.

After playing, I visited the Acro yoga group and said hello to Toliver a bit. Miyako reminded me that she hadn't bought a copy of my book so she came over to buy one. Prasun bought one as well. Happy and grateful! 

After Miyako left, we packed up and six of us went to Jangara https://maps.app.goo.gl/v17ZJXqqn8osBAEm8

Andrew, Julia (sp (trust me)), Mike, Prasun, Jack, and I were there for nearly an hour enjoying yum-yum vegan options.

Prasun had to leave early then Jack had to go to Shibuya, but then the rest of us also went to Shibuya to check out some pastry place that Andrew and Julia wanted to see. 

Mike bought us all some custard type things that were served in actual eggshells. I had one; (not vegan, but) it was yummy.

I fell asleep on the Inokashira Line and Odakyu Line but was able to wake up correctly at my stations. Yayyyyyyy 🙌
