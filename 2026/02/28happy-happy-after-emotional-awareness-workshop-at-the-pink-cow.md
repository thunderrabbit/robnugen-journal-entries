My emotional awareness workshop on February 28th at The Pink Cow went wonderfully better than I had imagined!

18 people attended the workshop portion!  There was some delay with late arrivals and making sure everyone had placed their orders, during which time I could feel irritation arise.  I held space for myself and allowed it to pass.  With everyone chatting and meeting each other, it was relatively easy to see that everyone was having a great time, and I was the only one worried about timing.

And, with everyone chatting and meeting each other, it was relatively loud so I got everyone's attention by tinking a spoon against a glass.

Most important, despite being super excited and anxious to get started, I remembered to thank everyone for coming and express how happy I felt to have all my favorite people get to meet each other!

The workshop portion was super easy to present, especially after I started with a grounding meditation, focusing on the five common senses.  

After the grounding meditation, I skipped the next two planned bits: 1. Go over the confidentiality agreements (because I planned to skip #2). 2. Each person check-in (because everybody had already met each other and we would definitely have no time remaining for the workshop).

Instead, I jumped straight into the physical activity, basically: "stand up (carefully), close your eyes, stretch your arms, wiggle your fingers, sit down (carefully)."  The intention was to engage some not so commonly enumerated senses, e.g., balance, proprioception, interoception.

We were low on time, and there were native Japanese ESL attendees, so those three senses remained unenumerated during the workshop. 😂 

I therefore also didn't talk about emotions as (even less often enumerated) senses, but I did talk about how our emotions are affected by interacting in public, and that if we get stress from interacting with each other, we need to process that stress, otherwise we'll spread stress to other people.

After that, I quickly presented the confidentiality agreements: no advice, yes privacy.  Once everyone agreed, we went to the paired share questions.  Basically summarizeable as "How do you do self-care?"

After that, I presented a story from my book, featuring my rarely heard Texan accent.  Everyone clapped as we finished right on time.

Note to self: next time you do a book launch, remember to let attendees know you have books available!
