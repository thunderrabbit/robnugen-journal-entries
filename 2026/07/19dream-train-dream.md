<p class="dream">
We were on a train. That was like going somewhere? I knew the
driver. And was talking to him about whatever and we needed to
stop. He drove up a hill. And then let the train kind of slide
backwards down the hill. To get to the right stopping place. And
when he stopped and opened the doors, we were able to get out, but
the train alignment wasn't with the station. We had to climb out
onto the rocks inside of the train tracks.
</p>

<p class="dream">
I helped some people get out of the train, and then we were
walking around on the rocks next to the train. And I saw a
different train and I realized, oh, I should have François and
get some pictures, so I climbed back into the first train to look
for my camera.
</p>

<p class="dream">
But I couldn't find my camera. And I prayed for support to get
it. But ended up waking up. Instead of finding my camera, and then
I realized I can't take photos in dreams so that was funny.
</p>
