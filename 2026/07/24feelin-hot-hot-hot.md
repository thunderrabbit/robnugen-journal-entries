I just went to the beach to go rock hopping .. rock hopping was the easy part!  The sand is already hot enough that I had to run from the sidewalk to the rocks!

The thicker soles of my feet can handle the heat, but the sand is squishy and goes up into the underside of my toes where they generally don't touch the ground at all so the skin is quite thin.

I will definitely be telling people at this coming Sunday beach bootcamp to wear shoes when they go on the sand! Last year some people tried barefoot and ended up just crying in pain.  The weather forecast currently predicts that it's going to be overcast; I hope it's correct!
