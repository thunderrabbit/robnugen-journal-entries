Ayako 先生 and Mio are back in Tokyo for a bit so we all hung out in Yoyogi Park at the new Doutor Cafe that opened in April while I was in OZ.

The cafe is rebuilt, modeled on the 1946 building from occupation era and then the Dutch dorm for Olympics in 1964.

Mio wanted to race everyone at our table.  I accepted her offer a few times.  She was able to jump rope up to 12 times apparently.  She said it's easier for her to jump high, though it seems to me it uses a lot of energy.  I was able to jump about 23 or 24 times.
