<p class="dream">I was staying at Misa's room in her house,
which I had been there for a while and realized that when I
go back to Japan I can just live there instead of living alone
in my apartment. And in this house it was quite big and she had
an extra room in a very large living room, dining area that I hadn't
even seen yet. And we talked about it and Misa said it was a good idea
and I was welcome to live there anytime. So I gave her a hug and then
I got a call on my laptop via WhatsApp and I could see it was from Heidi,
who I knew from church back in the late 1990s. And she was calling to
have what she said a check in, but it turns out she was feeling very
depressed or sad about her situation because someone had installed had
caught and stuffed a large shark in the entryway of her house so she
could no longer see out her window at the next train station,
which was Tambelin Station. And she was feeling sad about that. And when she
was talking, her father who had installed the shark was saying that she
shouldn't talk to people about it. So I told her that it was all confidential.
And I realized, because I was at Misa's house already, that Heidi was
just next door. And I was surprised that I had forgotten that she was
just next door last time I was at Misa's house. So I was thinking to
tell her, to ask her if she was there and tell her we could go for a
walk instead of talk on the phone.</p>
