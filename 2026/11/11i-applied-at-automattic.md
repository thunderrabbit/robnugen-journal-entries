## Tell us about a project or piece of work you’re especially proud of 

TL;DR: AI and I are building a bilingual client website while emailing the client in Japanese language.

A few years ago (pre-AI-era), I created a template[1] and process[2] for launching websites on Dreamhost Shared Hosting. I could spin up a new user on DH with a new domain in about an hour, and then begin manually working on the project in VSCode (typing with my fingers like a pre-ai-caveman). 

Integrating this template and process with AI-agents (Claude Code), I have been able to work with a Japanese client: a cronjob runs every five minutes on my local machine, pings me on Telegram if the client wrote to me. Then I can sit at my terminal monitor Claude in translating the email and doing Test Driven Dev to make the changes to the site. Because of the magic of AI, I've been able to produce up-to-date documentation for the site in multiple languages and formats. This was nigh impossible just a couple years ago for me as a solo dev, especially while not able to speak Japanese as business level!

[1] https://github.com/thunderrabbit/new-DH-user-site
[2] https://github.com/thunderrabbit/new-DH-user-account
